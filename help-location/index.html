Online Inventory Management Software

Location is an online inventory and warehouse management service designed to help businesses organize products, record stock movements, and maintain warehouse documentation.

Create a digital warehouse, manage your product catalog, record incoming and outgoing stock, and monitor inventory levels. Use warehouse documents to keep track of stock movements and access reports to review your inventory.

Location also supports importing and exporting product catalogs and warehouse documents, helping you transfer and manage your data more efficiently.

In addition to inventory management, Location allows you to publish product listings that are visible to other users. Potential buyers can contact you by email to discuss purchases. Currently, payments and transactions are arranged directly between buyers and sellers outside the Location platform.

Location is designed to evolve into a platform that can combine inventory management with an integrated online store.
